# Methodology

The workflow begins with a field audit and excludes concepts that the extract does not observe. Financial exposure appears on the value axis. It is not included in the observable risk score, preventing the same line-item value from driving both axes.

The score uses three eligible signals: late-rate percentile (45%), positive commercial variance within exact item/form/pack/country/year cohorts (35%), and the supplier’s highest observed share inside one item description (20%). A supplier must have at least 10 shipments and 10 comparable lines before a score and action are produced.

Schedule results are labelled a **schedule-adherence proxy**. The source warns that direct lead-time or product/country cost conclusions can be misleading; this project therefore does not claim supplier SLA performance or lead-time risk. Commercial variance is a screening signal, never a savings estimate.

Actions are evidence gates: `RENEGOTIATE` requires comparable commercial evidence; `DEVELOP` requires delivery evidence; `DIVERSIFY` requires both high observable risk and material value share. The labels are portfolio hypotheses for review, not instructions to switch suppliers.
