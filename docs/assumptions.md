# Assumptions and limits

- Dates are parsed as `DD-MM-YYYY`, matching the downloaded extract.
- A designated intermediary-labelled vendor record is treated as non-external and is retained in data-quality totals but excluded from external supplier actions.
- Exact product/form/pack/country/year cohorts reduce, but do not remove, commercial comparability bias.
- Source years are historical (2006–2015). The results do not describe current market conditions.
- Missing quality and compliance attributes are exclusions, not imputed risks.
